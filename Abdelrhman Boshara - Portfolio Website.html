About Me

I am a first-year student at the University of California, Los Angeles studying Computational Biology with a concentration in Bioinformatics. I am drawn to the overlap between data science and molecular biology — particularly how computational tools can reveal patterns in genomic data that drive biological understanding.

Currently I work as a Bioinformatics Research Assistant in the Jessica Rexach Lab, where I run bulk RNA-seq pipelines and differential gene expression analyses. Alongside research, I am on the Software Team for the Rocket Project at UCLA and volunteer as a STEM educator with BEAM.

I am fluent in both Arabic and English and bring experience working in fast-paced, team-oriented environments.
